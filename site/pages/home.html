How it works

Three steps.

  1. 1
    Open a streamOn Kick or Twitch, in your own browser tab. Press the Link button and the panel opens on the page.
  2. 2
    Press RecordMark a highlight with a hotkey whenever something happens. Stop when you are done.
  3. 3
    Get one file with your marksAn mp4 in your Downloads folder, and the list of marks ready for your editor.

Everything about Link being open source

Use it for streams you have the right to record.

Link records what is playing in your own tab. It is made for your own stream, streams you have permission to clip, and work you do for a streamer. The recording stays on your computer. Following the rules of the platform you record from, and what you do with the file, are your responsibility.
