# Code of conduct

Link's issues, pull requests and Discord server are for people who make clips and people who make
Link. Everyone is welcome who behaves like a guest in somebody else's house.

## What we ask

- Be kind. Disagree with what was said, not with who said it.
- Stay on the subject of the issue or the channel.
- Assume the other person means well, and is perhaps not writing in their first language.
- Take "no" for an answer: not every idea fits, and one person makes Link.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment, or remarks about someone's origin, gender, body, age, religion or orientation.
- Publishing someone's private details.
- Spam, and advertising that has nothing to do with Link.
- Asking for help with recording or posting material you have no right to use.

## What happens

A comment that breaks this is removed. Someone who keeps at it is blocked from the repository and the
server. To report something, write to **support@linkturbo.app**; what you write stays between us.

This text follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), in
fewer words.
