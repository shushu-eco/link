# Link: copyright complaints

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Link is a free, open-source tool for making clips from streams and videos. People who use it are responsible for having
the right to what they record and post. We do not host their recordings or clips: those stay on their
own computers and on the platforms they post to.

## If your content was posted in Link's Discord server

Write to support@linkturbo.app or message a moderator in the server, and say:

1. who you are, and that you own the content or act for the owner;
2. what the content is (for example the stream or video it was taken from);
3. where it is in the server (a link to the message);
4. how we can reach you.

We remove it promptly, and tell the member who posted it. Members who post other people's content
repeatedly are removed from the server.

## If a clip made with Link was posted somewhere else

We cannot remove content from YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Kick or Twitch. Use that platform's own
copyright complaint form; it is the fastest way and the only one that works. You are still welcome to
tell us at the address above, so that we know.

## If you are a streamer and do not want your streams recorded with Link

Write to the address above. We will tell you what is possible.
